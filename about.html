Our mission

Paperwork is our problem — not yours.

Fyrm handles the paperwork of starting and running a company — name search, incorporation, minute book, government filings, and deadline tracking, all from one dashboard. Filed with Corporations Canada and all 13 provincial & territorial registries. You build the business; we keep it in good standing.

What we stand by.

Three principles behind everything Fyrm does.

01
Plain language over legalese
Government forms ask questions in government words. We ask them in yours — and turn your answers into filings the registry accepts.
02
Done for you, not handed to you
No templates, no homework. We prepare, file, and track everything ourselves — your only job is to run the business.
03
Built for Canada
We file with Corporations Canada and all 13 provincial & territorial registries. Canadian incorporation isn't a feature of Fyrm — it's the whole company.
