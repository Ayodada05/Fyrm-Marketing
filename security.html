Security

Security at Fyrm.

A filings platform holds the details of your company. Here is how we think about protecting them.

Last updated: July 2026

This is a pre-launch draft; it will be finalized before public launch.

Our approach

Fyrm prepares and files the documents that define your company — its articles, its directors, its registered office. Information like that deserves better than an afterthought, so security is a foundation of the product, not a feature we add later.

In practice, that means we design with a simple bias: collect less, restrict access, and keep the boundaries between systems clear. We would rather not hold information than hold it carelessly.

Encryption

Connections to Fyrm are encrypted in transit. The marketing site you are reading and the Fyrm product are served over HTTPS, so information moving between your browser and us is protected on the way.

Access

We follow the principle of least privilege. Access to systems and to customer information is limited to the people who need it to do their work, granted narrowly, and removed when it is no longer needed.

Data

We collect only what your filings require, and this marketing site collects nothing on its own. For what we collect, how we use it, and how long we keep it, see our privacy policy and our PIPEDA commitments.

Reporting a concern

If you believe you have found a security issue in anything Fyrm operates, we want to hear about it — responsible disclosure is welcome, and we will take your report seriously. Please reach us through the contact page and include enough detail for us to reproduce what you found.
