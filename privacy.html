Privacy

Privacy policy

How we handle personal information — what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Written in plain language, like everything else we do.

Last updated: July 2026

This is a pre-launch draft; it will be finalized before public launch.

The short version

We collect as little personal information as possible, use it only to incorporate your business and keep it compliant, and never sell it. This policy belongs to Fyrm Technologies Inc. The sections below spell out exactly what all of that means.

What we collect

Today, this marketing site collects nothing on its own. It is a set of static pages with no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no forms — reading it sends us no personal information.

When the Fyrm product launches, this policy will govern the information you provide to use it:

  • Account information — your name and the contact details you sign up with.
  • Company information — the details needed to run a name search, prepare your articles, and file with Corporations Canada or a provincial or territorial registry, such as your company's name, address, directors, and share structure.
  • Records we maintain for you — your minute book, your filed documents, and the deadlines we track on your behalf.

How we use information

We use personal information for one purpose: providing Fyrm. That means preparing and submitting the government filings you ask for, maintaining your minute book and corporate records, tracking your deadlines so you never miss a filing again, and supporting you when you have questions. Your information goes to a government registry only as part of a filing you have asked us to make.

What we never do

We never sell personal information. We do not rent it, trade it, or share it with third parties for their own marketing. That will not change.

Retention & access

Corporate records exist to be kept — so we retain your filings and minute book for as long as you use Fyrm, and afterwards for as long as the law requires. Beyond that, we keep personal information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above.

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete anything we are not legally required to keep. Reach us through our contact page and we will respond.

PIPEDA

We follow the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Canada's federal private-sector privacy law. Our commitments under its ten fair information principles — from accountability to individual access — are set out on our PIPEDA page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about how your information is handled, can reach us through our contact page. We will update this policy as the product takes shape, and the date at the top will always tell you when it last changed.
