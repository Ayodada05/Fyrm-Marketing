Questions about the process.

The three things founders usually ask before they start — the full list lives on the FAQ page.

How do I choose where to incorporate?

It mostly comes down to where you operate and how widely you want your name protected. We file with Corporations Canada and all 13 provincial & territorial registries, so either path works — and we walk you through the choice in plain language as you answer the questions.

What happens after I'm incorporated?

Your documents and minute book land in your dashboard, and Fyrm keeps working — deadlines are tracked and ongoing filings are prepared and submitted for you, so you never miss a filing again.

What if the registry needs more from me?

Sometimes a registry asks for a correction or an extra detail. When that happens, we tell you exactly what's needed, handle the resubmission, and keep you posted until it's done.
