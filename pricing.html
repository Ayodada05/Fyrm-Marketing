Pricing questions, answered.

What founders ask about plans, fees, and the fine print.

Are government fees included in the plan price?

No — government filing fees are set by each registry and vary by jurisdiction, so they're charged separately from your plan. We always show you the exact fee before you confirm anything.

Can I switch plans later?

Our plans are still being finalized ahead of launch, so we won't promise specifics yet — but we're designing them so you can move between plans as your business changes. Final details will be announced at launch.

Is there a contract or a cancellation fee?

The final terms are being written alongside the plans, so we won't guess here. Our intent is simple — pricing that stays out of your way, with nothing designed to lock you in. Full terms will be published at launch.

What does the $1 mean?

It's a placeholder, not a price. Our final plans and prices haven't been announced yet — the $1 simply marks where the real numbers will go when we launch. Nothing is charged today.
